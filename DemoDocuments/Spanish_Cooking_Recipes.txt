TORTILLA ESPANOLA (Spanish Omelette)

The tortilla espanola is Spain's most iconic dish. It's a thick potato omelette that can be served hot or cold, as a tapa or a main course.

Ingredients (serves 4):
- 6 large eggs
- 4 medium potatoes (about 500g), peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced (optional but recommended)
- 200ml olive oil for frying
- Salt to taste

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a deep frying pan over medium heat.
2. Add potato slices and onion, cook slowly for 20-25 minutes until soft but not browned. Stir occasionally.
3. Drain potatoes and onion, reserving the oil. Let cool slightly.
4. Beat eggs in a large bowl with salt. Add the potato-onion mixture.
5. Heat 2 tablespoons of the reserved oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
6. Pour in the egg mixture. Cook for 3-4 minutes until the bottom sets.
7. Place a plate over the pan and flip the tortilla. Slide it back into the pan.
8. Cook for another 2-3 minutes. The center should be slightly juicy (jugosa).
9. Serve warm or at room temperature, cut into wedges.

The key to a perfect tortilla is cooking the potatoes slowly in olive oil. They should be tender, almost falling apart. Never rush this step.

GAZPACHO ANDALUZ

Gazpacho is a cold tomato soup from Andalusia, perfect for hot summer days. It requires no cooking.

Ingredients (serves 4):
- 1 kg ripe tomatoes
- 1 green pepper
- 1 cucumber
- 1 clove garlic
- 100ml extra virgin olive oil
- 30ml sherry vinegar
- 200ml water
- Salt to taste
- Stale bread (optional, about 100g, for thickness)

Instructions:
1. Roughly chop all vegetables.
2. If using bread, soak it in water for 5 minutes.
3. Blend everything together until completely smooth.
4. Pass through a fine sieve for a silky texture (optional).
5. Season with salt and add more vinegar if needed.
6. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
7. Serve cold with diced cucumber, pepper, and croutons as garnish.
8. Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil before serving.

Traditional gazpacho should be smooth and drinkable. Avoid adding too many vegetables or it becomes salmorejo (a thicker cousin from Cordoba).

PAELLA VALENCIANA

Authentic paella comes from Valencia and traditionally uses rabbit, chicken, and beans. Seafood paella is a coastal adaptation.

Ingredients (serves 6):
- 400g bomba or calasparra rice (never rinse it)
- 300g chicken, cut into pieces
- 200g rabbit, cut into pieces
- 150g green beans (judias verdes)
- 100g garrofon beans (or butter beans)
- 1 large tomato, grated
- 1 liter chicken broth
- Saffron threads (about 0.5g)
- 2 tablespoons sweet paprika (pimenton dulce)
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh rosemary sprig

Instructions:
1. Heat olive oil in a paella pan (about 40cm wide) over high heat.
2. Brown the chicken and rabbit pieces on all sides. Season with salt.
3. Add green beans and garrofon, cook for 2 minutes.
4. Push everything to the sides, add grated tomato in the center. Cook until darkened.
5. Add paprika, stir quickly (don't let it burn), then add the broth.
6. Add saffron and rosemary. Bring to a boil and cook for 20 minutes.
7. Taste the broth and adjust salt. It should be slightly over-seasoned because the rice will absorb flavor.
8. Add rice in a cross or line pattern, distributing evenly. Do not stir after this point.
9. Cook on high for 7 minutes, then reduce to medium for 13 minutes.
10. The perfect paella has "socarrat" - a crispy caramelized rice layer on the bottom.
11. Remove from heat, cover with a cloth, and rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Never stir paella once the rice is added. The socarrat is the soul of the dish. If you hear a slight crackling sound from the bottom in the last minutes, you're doing it right.
